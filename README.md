Privacy Policy – Liftlytics

Last updated: 19 February 2026

Liftlytics (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Liftlytics mobile application.

⸻

1. Information We Collect

When you use Liftlytics, we may collect the following information:

Personal Information
	•	Name (display name)
	•	Email address (used for authentication)
	•	Profile photo (optional)

Fitness & App Data
	•	Workout logs (exercises, sets, reps, weights)
	•	Analytics derived from workouts (volume, progress, personal records)
	•	App usage data required for core functionality

Authentication Data
	•	Email & password or Google Sign-In (handled securely via Firebase Authentication)

⸻

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:
	•	Create and manage your Liftlytics account
	•	Store and display your workouts and analytics
	•	Personalize your experience
	•	Improve app performance and reliability

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

⸻

3. Data Storage & Security
	•	All data is securely stored using Google Firebase (Firestore & Firebase Storage).
	•	Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.
	•	Industry-standard security measures are used to protect your data.

⸻

4. Data Sharing

Liftlytics does not share your personal or fitness data with:
	•	Advertisers
	•	Data brokers
	•	Third-party analytics platforms

⸻
